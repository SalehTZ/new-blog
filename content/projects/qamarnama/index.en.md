A Persian-language lunar calendar app built around one question: *"نکنه امروز قمر در عقربه؟"* — "is the moon in Scorpio today?"

<!--more-->

{{< badge >}}Released{{< /badge >}}

QamarNama tracks when the moon enters Scorpio and surfaces those dates on a Jalali calendar with a minimal, at-a-glance interface — a traditional consideration in Persian folk astrology and day-to-day planning.
